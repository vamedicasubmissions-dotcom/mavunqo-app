# Thursday Lawyer & DR Liability Playbook (Phone Edition)

## Part 1: Dominican Republic Liability Check (What to Lock Down)
Under Dominican Republic law, three rules matter for a booking platform:
1. **Law No. 63-17 (Transit & Vehicles):** Every circulating car, buggy, or scooter must have a valid *marbete*, *matrícula*, and mandatory *Seguro de Ley* (third-party insurance).
2. **Law No. 358-05 (Pro Consumidor):** Public-policy consumer protection law. Abusive or hidden waivers can be voided, and platforms that fail to vet suppliers or present themselves as the direct provider can face Pro Consumidor scrutiny.
3. **Civil Code Art. 1382–1384 (*Guardián de la Cosa*):** Civil responsibility follows whoever has custody/control of the vehicle—and victims can name multiple parties if roles aren't clearly separated.

### What to Have the Lawyer Draft for Mavunqo
* **Intermediary-Only Terms of Service:** Explicitly state Mavunqo is a digital marketplace/booking intermediary—**never** the tour operator, carrier, or vehicle owner.
* **Operator Verification & Insurance Clause:** Every operator/rental partner must warrant they hold valid *Seguro de Ley*, *marbete*, permits, and their own RNC, and sign an **indemnification clause** holding Mavunqo harmless for accidents or injuries.
* **Self-Drive Rule (Cars & Scooters):** Keep self-drive rentals off at launch—or require a separate operator-signed rental contract, ID/license check, and security deposit strictly between customer and operator.

## Part 5: Red Flags to Avoid & 5 Questions for Thursday
### Avoid These
* Launching **self-drive scooter/car rentals** without verified *Seguro de Ley*, *marbete*, and a strict intermediary shield.
* Presenting yourself as the **direct seller/operator** instead of a booking intermediary.
* Taking 100% of funds as your own revenue without separating operator payouts for **DGII / ITBIS (18%)**.

### Ask the Lawyer These 5 Questions
1. **Entity:** Should Mavunqo be an **SRL** or **EIRL** in the DR for a tech intermediary platform?
2. **Liability Shield:** How do we word our **Terms of Service** under **Law 358-05 (Pro Consumidor)** and **Civil Code Art. 1384** so Mavunqo is not treated as the *guardián de la cosa* or direct operator?
3. **Operator Contract:** Can you draft a **1-page Vendor / Tour Operator Agreement** with an indemnification clause and mandatory *Seguro de Ley* / RNC verification?
4. **Taxes (DGII / ITBIS 18%):** When we collect a **20%–25% deposit** and the guest pays the rest in cash to the operator, how do we structure receipts so we're only taxed on our platform fee?
5. **Future Expansion:** What extra permits or insurance clauses are needed before ever turning on **self-drive rentals** or **driver ride coordination**?
