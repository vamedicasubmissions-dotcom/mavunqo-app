📋 20-Second Call Script & North Coast Scouting Guide

Spanish Pitch: "Hola, llamo de MAVUNQO.com en Sosúa. Conectamos restaurantes, tours, masajes y villas con turistas sin costo mensual—solo ganamos si les llevamos clientes. ¿Le envío el enlace de 2 minutos por WhatsApp?"

English Pitch: "Hi, I'm calling from MAVUNQO.com. We bring North Coast tourists to local restaurants, tours, massages, and villas with zero monthly fee. Can I text you our 2-minute signup link?"

B2B & SaaS Pitch (ES): "Hola, soy de MAVUNQO.com. Ayudamos a negocios y plataformas locales a automatizar reservas y depender menos de Airbnb o OTAs que cobran 15%—solo cobramos 15% cuando nosotros mismos les llevamos clientes nuevos. ¿Podemos agendar una llamada rápida?"

B2B & SaaS Pitch (EN): "Hi, I'm with MAVUNQO.com. We help local businesses, suppliers, and platforms automate their workflow and wean off 15% Airbnb/OTA fees—you only pay 15% on new customers we bring you directly. Open to a quick 5-minute chat?"

Where to Scout & What to Look For (Cast a wide net first, then double down on what gets traction):
  • Sosúa (El Batey / Sosúa Ocean Village / Playa Alicia): Tourist-friendly restaurants, colmados with delivery, mobile massage therapists, villa hosts, catamaran & snorkel operators.
  • Cabarete (Main Strip / Kite Beach / Encuentro): Surf & kite schools, beachfront cafes, healthy food spots, scooter/car rentals.
  • Puerto Plata & Montellano (Malecón / Cofresí / Cruise Port): Excursion operators (27 Waterfalls, Teleférico, ATV/buggy, Del Oro Chocolate), private drivers, spas.
  • B2B & SaaS Distribution: Local hospitality suppliers, POS/booking software vendors, property management companies, and wholesale distributors serving North Coast businesses.
  • Supply-Chain Chain-Reaction (All Clients): Whenever any business signs up (hotel, restaurant, tour, or supplier), ask for their top suppliers/distributors—and when you sign that supplier, ask for their suppliers too—adding each one as a new lead right here.
